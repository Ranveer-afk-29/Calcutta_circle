Membership

Two chapters, one table

Membership is by application and subject to the Circle's discretion. Both chapters carry full calendar access; the distinction lies in standing and privilege.

Mentor Member
By invitation · Annual renewal
  • Reserved for established connoisseurs who guide tastings and curate sessions
  • Featured profile on the Circle's Mentor page (with consent)
  • First right to host and curate the monthly tasting
  • Priority invitation to member trips
Enquire on Mentor Standing
Privilege Member
By invitation · Annual renewal
  • Open to applicants who meet the Circle's eligibility criteria
  • Full access to monthly tastings and the events calendar
  • Booking priority for the Annual Celebration and trips
  • Digital membership card and self-service portal access
  • Pathway to Mentor standing by nomination
Enquire on Member Standing
