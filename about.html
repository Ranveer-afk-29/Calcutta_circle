About the Circle

Born of a simple conviction

That good taste is best kept in company — and that a city with Calcutta's appetite for conversation deserved a table of its own.

Origin

The Circle was convened under The Sovereign Table Holdings as a private society for those who take their tastings, and their company, seriously. It exists apart from the city's clubs and restaurants — smaller, slower, and built around a shared calendar rather than a shared address.

Philosophy

We hold that connoisseurship is a practice, not a possession. Every gathering is designed to teach as much as it indulges — guided by mentors, hosted by partners who share the same regard for craft, and kept deliberately small.

"We did not set out to build a club. We set out to keep a table — one we would want to return to ourselves, month after month, for the rest of our lives. Everything about the Circle, its size, its pace, its guest list, follows from that." — Founders' Note, The Sovereign Table Holdings
